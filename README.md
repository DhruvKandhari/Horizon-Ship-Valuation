# 🚢 Horizon Ship Price Valuation – Predictive Analytics in Maritime Industry

### 👋 About This Project
In this project, I performed a detailed valuation analysis of maritime vessels for Horizon Inc., a company specializing in ship brokerage, recycling, and asset management. The goal was straightforward but challenging: use data-driven approaches to predict accurate vessel valuations and deliver strategic bidding recommendations.

### 🎯 What I Aimed to Achieve
- Predict accurate ship valuations using historical market data.
- Identify and analyze factors significantly impacting vessel pricing.
- Provide strategic insights for making competitive market offers.

### 🛠️ How I Did It (Skills & Tools Used)
- **Data Analysis & Visualization** *(Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib)*
- **Statistical Modeling** *(Linear Regression, Predictive Analytics)*
- **Market Research & Benchmarking** *(Industry Reports, Clarkson Research, Vessel Value)*
- **Presentation & Communication** *(PowerPoint, Excel)*

### 📈 What I Found
- The best predictors for vessel pricing were Age, Deadweight Tonnage (DWT), and the Capesize Index.
- Our predictive regression model achieved an impressive accuracy (R² = 92.5%).
- The recommended optimal value for the targeted ship, "Bet Performer," was **$75.13 million**.

### 🌟 Recommendations Delivered
- Suggested bidding below **$90 million** based on predictive insights, well below typical market prices (~$125M), offering strategic savings.
